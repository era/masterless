The year 2015 was a crazy year for me, it started with a great Foo Fighters show at Morumbi, and end up with me living in Ireland. Almost every year, several teams from Amazon go to Brasil and other American countries to hire as many engineers as they can. They initially send a programming exercise by email, those who pass are invited to an interview (a whole day of it).

In January, I received one of those exercises, the Amazon retail team from Seattle was going to be in São Paulo, and they invited me to participate in the process. The first test was defined in a way that you needed Dynamic Programming to solve it (what was the most common sequence of pages visited by customers). I had just finished the Complexity of Algorithms class for my Master’s, so I was able to solve it.

They invited me to go to São Paulo for a whole afternoon of interviews. Since São Paulo is a bit far from Campinas (especially if you go by bus), I asked them if I could sleep in the hotel on the previous night (they were offering this to anyone that lived far from the city). On the night before my interview, there was a meet up between all the interviewees and the Amazon workers. We talked about everything, about work, life in Seattle, and Brasil.

I was not sure if I wanted to move abroad. I love living in Brasil and although I was not hopeful of getting better salaries in the country, I still had family and friends around me there. But I really wanted to work creating tech instead of just using it. What did change my mind was the hotel room. It was a fancy hotel, I have never been to such a place. They had a bathtub! I have never used one before, I felt like in the movies. That convinced me, I wanted to be rich, to be able to live a comfortable material life. To be fair, after moving to Ireland, I discovered what I called “been rich” is just what Europeans call “middle-class”. Like, being able to buy books, travel to other countries, have a nice computer, buying a video-game without spending all your savings.

I passed the interview, they called me and explained how much I would make. I thought I didn’t understand, I waited for the email with the numbers. It was true, I was going to make per year more money I thought I was ever going to be able to hold in my hands. I received a signing bonus (some money just because I was joining the company), some money to help to move (more money I ever had) and a lot of stocks. I got lucky, Amazon stocks were worth around 300 dollars when I joined the company. I was going to move to Seattle to work for Amazon retail.

For those who don’t know, the visa process to work in the USA is broken. They have a lottery to decide the applications they will look at. You have to provide all types of weird documents, like your college grades. In the end, I was not picked at the lottery.  Amazon called me, they said I could choose work for AWS in Dublin or Germany.  I picked Dublin.  The offer was smaller, much less money per year, although the same bonuses and stocks. One big advantage though, life in Europe is better than in the USA. Better life-work balance, worker's rights, more secure (not only talking about crimes here, but I could change jobs much easier than with the USA visa rules).

I had to wait 6 months to know I wasn’t going to move to Seattle, the whole process to live in Dublin took 2 months.
